TTD HQ // DATA DOCTRINE

PRIVACY

TTD HQ is a mostly static web app. This page explains the limited information that may be handled when you browse, play, contact us or use an external payment rail.

WHAT THE SITE MAY HANDLE

  • Basic technical request data handled by the hosting/network providers needed to deliver the site.
  • Local browser storage used by some games or site features for settings, progress, scores or interface state.
  • Information you voluntarily send by email, including your email address and message content.
  • Information handled by third-party payment services when you choose to use those services.

WHAT TTD HQ DOES NOT NEED

The public GitHub Pages build does not require you to create a TTD account to read comics or play the current browser games.

Private payment credentials are not stored in the public website source. Payment providers may have their own privacy practices and terms.

COOKIES / LOCAL STORAGE

Some browser features and games may use local storage or similar browser storage for game progress, high scores, preferences or installable-web-app behaviour. Clearing browser/site data may remove that locally stored information.

There is currently no dedicated advertising tracker intentionally activated by this site-finish package.

EMAIL AND SUPPORT

If you email TTD HQ, the message and contact details you provide may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve an order/support issue, maintain necessary business records, or meet legal obligations.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

TTD HQ may link to or use third-party services such as hosting/CDN infrastructure, PayPal, Bitcoin Lightning services, social platforms or other external providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Privacy questions can be sent to TTDHQ@proton.me.

This notice describes the current lightweight site. If TTD HQ later adds accounts, a cart, mailing list, dedicated analytics or another data-heavy feature, this privacy notice should be updated before that feature goes live.
